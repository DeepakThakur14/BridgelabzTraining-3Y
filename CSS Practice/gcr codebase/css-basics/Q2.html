The Pink City

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan and is popularly known as the Pink City. It is famous for its beautiful palaces, forts, markets and rich cultural heritage.

History of Jaipur

Jaipur was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II. It was one of the earliest planned cities of India. The city was designed according to principles of architecture and urban planning.

The city became famous for its pink-colored buildings and has remained an important historical and cultural destination.

Famous Places

Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is one of Jaipur's most famous landmarks, known for its unique windows and beautiful architecture.

Amber Fort

Amber Fort is a historic fort famous for its magnificent architecture and hilltop location.

City Palace

City Palace is a beautiful complex containing palaces, courtyards, museums and royal buildings.

Culture of Jaipur

Jaipur has a rich culture that includes traditional music, dance, handicrafts, colourful clothing and delicious Rajasthani food.

The city attracts visitors with its festivals, traditional markets and warm hospitality.
